I was happy for the opportunity to build a remote-first engineering team from scratch. And ensuring we are putting end users at the forefront of our activities was a rewarding process I had too little exposure to until now. I think my time at Vamo has taught me a few lessons in project management within a very different type of organization which I find very valuable. Finally, it renewed my commitment to joining an international team where each culture represented finds a way to positively impact the company culture as a whole.
\vspace{.5em}
\par I would be delighted to discuss whether an opportunity exists should you still find my expertise relevant, and I thank you for considering my application. I remain at your disposal for any further information.
